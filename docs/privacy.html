Privacy information · Updated November 8, 2026

Local solo play

Game progress, settings, local notes and a local multiplayer device identifier are stored on your device. The game contains no advertising, tracking or analytics SDK and does not require a game account. Solo gameplay does not send saves to a developer server.

Optional nearby multiplayer

Joining a nearby Bluetooth room shares game state, player names and messages with participating devices. The hosting device simulates and stores that session. Bluetooth permission is optional for solo play. Older Android devices may require location permission for Bluetooth discovery; the game does not use GPS coordinates.

Backups and deletion

You may export or import a JSON save through Settings. Backups contain game progress and local messages; keep them private. Reset Local Progress clears the active game save and its local recovery backup. Clearing browser/app storage or uninstalling the app may delete local progress; platform cloud backup is not promised.

Store purchase

The planned mobile release uses a one-time app purchase handled by Apple or Google. The game contains no in-app purchase or subscription system and does not receive your payment card information. Store operators process purchase/account information under their own policies. The preview does not process payments.

Support

The project repository is Corgi-coder99/Game-studio. Use its issue tracker for reproducible technical problems if it is publicly available. Do not post private saves or payment details. The publisher must provide a publicly accessible support/privacy URL and final legal contact details before store release.
