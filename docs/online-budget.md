# Online release budget — planning estimates

Prepared 9 November 2026. Figures are USD allowances, not live quotes or expected revenue. Registrar price/availability requests for heavenlymandate.com and .com pricing were blocked by the current network allowlist; domain availability is unverified. A domain example is not a trademark clearance or a reservation.

| Expense | Planning allowance |
| --- | --- |
| Standard, non-premium .com | $10–20/year; renewal may differ |
| Small test VPS | $60–180/year |
| Backup/storage/monitoring | $0–100/year |
| Apple Developer | Commonly $99/year; verify local enrollment pricing |
| Google Play registration | Commonly $25 once; verify local eligibility/pricing |
| Remote iOS builds without owning a Mac | $0–300/year allowance |
| Original art/audio, device tests and incident reserve | $100–300 allowance |

Start with roughly $100–300 for a small web test, or budget $500–1,000 for a cautious first-year launch including both stores and contingency. A small subsequent year may fit $250–700, but concurrency, traffic, support, backups and security work can raise costs. Development labor, tax, refunds, legal costs and paid marketing are excluded. Store and hosting invoices must be checked before enrollment or purchase.

The website and native apps share the same backend cost. Store discovery is an additional channel, not guaranteed traffic. At a hypothetical $4.99 sale price and an eligible 15% store commission, $4.2415 remains before tax/refunds. About 118–236 sales offset $500–1,000 of operating cash, excluding acquisition/development costs. Eligibility and actual commission can differ. This is break-even arithmetic, not a forecast or a profit probability. Measure store-page views, paid conversion, day-1/day-7 retention and support cost before committing to broad marketing. A one-time purchase must fund ongoing online service over subsequent years.
